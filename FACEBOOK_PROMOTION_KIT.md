## PART 6: AD SCHEDULING & BUDGET

### Weekly Budget Allocation
- **Budget:** KES 4,000-5,000 ($30-40 USD) per week
- **Best posting times:** 
  - 7-9 AM (teachers prepping for school)
  - 6-8 PM (teachers after school)
  - 12-1 PM (lunch break)

### Ad Rotation
- Week 1-2: Run Ad 1 (Pain Point)
- Week 3-4: Test Ad 2 (Results) and Ad 3 (Value)
- Based on performance, scale the winner

### KPIs to Monitor
- Cost per click (CPC) - target: KES 10-20
- Click-through rate (CTR) - target: 2-4%
- Cost per lead/sign-up - track this in Google Analytics

## FINAL TIPS

✅ **Post consistently** - 3-4 times per week minimum
✅ **Respond quickly** - Answer comments within 2 hours
✅ **Use video** - Videos get 3x more engagement than text
✅ **Be authentic** - Teachers respect real stories, not hype
✅ **Ask questions** - "What's your biggest challenge with CBC?" gets engagement
✅ **Show results** - Share how much time teachers save
✅ **Monitor competitors** - See what other edtech companies post
✅ **Test, measure, scale** - Run small experiments, measure results, do more of what works

Good luck! 🚀
